The honest snapshot

You understand AI governance better than most people charging to teach it. You have a trademarked framework, a clear point of view, and fifteen years of practice in a space that suddenly everyone needs.

When I ran your name and your firm through the data, though — zero brand presence in AI search across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. No active website found across five domain variations.

None of this is about credibility. AI tools cite sources. Your LinkedIn is partly blocked from bots, and your podcast appearances live on other hosts' domains. That's what the tools are working from. There's nothing stable for them to point to.

Why each tool gives different results
Gemini Searches Google live. Finds whatever Google has indexed — fragments from LinkedIn, podcast show notes.
ChatGPT Same idea, Bing search. Pieces together what it can find in real time.
Claude Doesn't search at all. Works from training data only. If you weren't in it, it draws a blank.
Perplexity Most honest of the four. Shows exactly where it found what and cites sources. Start here to see your actual footprint.

Your starting move.

in order — the sequence matters
Step 01
Build a website you own
Not a Linktree. Not just LinkedIn. A real domain with pages that AI tools can read and reference. This is the single unlock for everything else. The rest of this list doesn't move until this is done.
Step 02
Write pages that answer what your clients are asking AI
Not blog posts — answer pages. "What is AI governance for small businesses?" "Who owns content created with AI tools?" Each page should open with the short answer, then go deeper. That structure is exactly what AI tools extract and repeat back to users.
Step 03
Own Creative Sovereignty™ on the web
You coined this phrase. Right now anyone who searches it gets your LinkedIn at best, nothing at worst. A page on your site defining it makes you the only citable source. Zero competition — no one else can claim it.
Step 04
Put your credentials where AI can read them
JD, LL.M., 15 years of practice, speaking history, publications — structured on a page that's easy to parse. AI tools build authority from clear, readable signals. A well-formatted bio page does this. A LinkedIn bio field doesn't.
Step 05
Then run the actual audit
Once the site exists, SE Ranking can show which AI engines are citing who for your topics — and what specific changes would put you there instead. No point running it before Step 01 is done.
